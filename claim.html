Not listed yet? Suggest a business

A suggested business is never published by this form. It is recorded under My Claims as “New listing — pending (demo)” for review, and it does not appear in search or browse results.

Suggestion recorded — demo

What happens after a claim?

  • The claim waits as Pending review (demo) — in a production directory a reviewer would check the proof note.
  • The listing shows a Claim pending (demo) badge while it waits.
  • Your claims and suggestions are listed under My Claims & Enquiries.
