About this demo

What this is

The Gujranwala Gazette Directory is a fictional city directory built as a portfolio demo by MTS for BalanceBit Solutions. It shows the front end of an MVP-style local directory: search-led browsing, category and area filters, listing pages, enquiries and a claim-your-listing flow.

What is demo data

All 18 businesses are invented. Their names, addresses, phone numbers, ratings and review counts are fictional demo data, written for this portfolio piece — no rating or review count on any page describes a real business. Photographs are generated demo images representing each category, not photographs of the fictional businesses.

How the demo clock works

“Open now” needs a “now”, so this demo fixes one: , printed in the masthead of every page. Open and closed state is worked out from each listing's Sunday hours against that fixed time, and a business counts as closed at exactly its closing time. One listing (Glow Studio Salon) closes at 2:30 PM on Sundays, so at the demo time it is shown closed — that boundary is deliberate.

Where your entries go

Enquiries, claims, suggestions and WhatsApp clicks are stored only in this browser's local storage, on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and no real business receives anything. Suggested businesses are never published by the form — they wait as “New listing — pending (demo)” for a review that, in this demo, never comes.

What a production build would add

  • A verified listings database with owner-managed details.
  • A real map with verified location pins (this demo shows an honestly labelled placeholder panel instead).
  • Moderated reviews from real customers.
  • Claim verification by a human reviewer before any badge or edit goes live.
  • Delivery of enquiries to the business by email or WhatsApp.
